# Why 127 became 214, decomposed

The requirement count rose 1.68x on i2c_master_bit_ctrl. "More requirements" is
only good if it is more *content*, so this attributes every one of the +87.

## The units nest, which makes the comparison exact

The sentence pass only subdivides: **all 168 of n3-i2c's units nest inside
exactly one of c1-i2c's 65**. So each c1 unit can be asked what it produced in
each run, and the delta lands on a named paragraph rather than on an average.

| | c1 | n3 |
|---|---|---|
| old units carrying at least one requirement | 36 | 50 |
| requirements from units productive in BOTH | 126 | 152 |
| requirements from units c1 left EMPTY | — | **62** |
| requirements from a unit that went silent | 1 | 0 |

**+62 of the +87 — 71% — comes from 15 paragraphs c1 produced NOTHING from.**
Attributed to why c1 dropped them:

* **54 requirements from 14 paragraphs c1 marked `continues_previous`**, whose
  obligations the fold deleted (see `continuations.md`).
* **8 requirements from one paragraph whose c1 response was headless** — "15.
  Reset behavior: On asynchronous reset `nReset = 0`, the FSM returns to idle,
  output enables are released high, ..." — scraped into a bare obligation,
  defaulted to `kind="scaffolding"`, gate silent. **The transport defect is not
  new to n3: c1 had 1 of 65 (1.5%), n3 had 6 of 168 (3.6%).**

## The remaining +26 is finer granularity, and it is modest

35 paragraphs were productive in both runs: **126 -> 152**, or **+0.74
requirements per paragraph**, from a partition 2.6x finer. Per behavioural unit
the classifier splits LESS than before -- **2.54 obligations per unit on c1,
1.66 on n3** -- which is what a smaller unit should do.

## Duplication barely moved

Token-Jaccard clustering at 0.6 over each set:

| | requirements | distinct clusters | redundant |
|---|---|---|---|
| c1 | 127 | 115 | 12 (9%) |
| n3 | 214 | 189 | **25 (12%)** |

So 1.68x the requirements is **1.64x the distinct content**. The 3-point rise is
the specification's own redundancy showing through: it states the reset
behaviour twice (an asynchronous-reset bullet at 9410 and a command-FSM reset
paragraph at 10066), and each now yields its seven facts.

## Several requirements share a span, and that is the design

119 distinct spans carry 214 requirements; c1 had 127 spans for 127. The large
groups are enumerations, where each item is independently right or wrong:

* 9 on the feature-list sentence (START/STOP, WRITE, READ, busy, arbitration,
  stretching, synchronisation, sync, glitch filtering)
* 7 on "the FSM returns to idle, output enables are released high, command
  acknowledge is cleared, arbitration lost is cleared, busy is cleared, counters
  and filters are reset, ..."
* 5 on "internal state, counters, filtered inputs, bus-status flags, and the
  command FSM are reset on the rising edge of `clk`"

This is the whole-unit span rule working: all nine feature-list requirements now
quote the entire sentence, where c1 gave the ninth `" and glitch filtering."`
and nothing else.

## One residual defect the review found, and fixed

Span 3703-3928 carried 7 requirements under a quote that mixed `scl_i`
behaviour with the `sda_i` declaration. Cause: `_SENTENCE_CUT`'s lookahead
demanded `[A-Z0-9\`]` after the terminator, and a specification's own names are
lower case --

    scl_i:External I2C SCL line input ... and read sampling.
    sda_i:External I2C SDA line input ...

-- so the boundary before `sda_i` was refused and one unit ran from the middle
of the SCL entry into the SDA entry. The cut now also fires on a sentence end
followed by a NEWLINE, which is unambiguous where a mid-line one is not.

Purely additive, measured before landing: i2c **168 -> 172 units with every
existing unit start unchanged**, and zero splits, overlaps or word-carrying gaps
across all 273 specs in both corpora.

## The consequence for the artifact, stated plainly

`/home/user/runs/n3-i2c` was produced under the 168-unit partition and is one
partition behind. It must NOT be resumed in place: `run_unit` names its stage
`classify_{unit.start}`, and since every existing start is unchanged, a resume
would replay the response of a LONGER unit for the four units that were split.
The gate catches it -- those obligations run past the new `unit.length` and are
rejected as "span is not inside the unit" -- so it is loud rather than silent,
but the right move is a fresh run directory.
