# Brief: qualitative error analysis, generation-only vs repaired RTL

## The question
Two arms of designs were written for the same specifications by the same model:
- **generated** — RTL written once from the spec and the run's contract, no repair. Per corpus: 3 ablation
  samples (`gen1..gen3`) plus the RTL Editor's own generated design before repair (`r0`).
- **repaired** — the RTL Editor's final design (`final`): `r0` after up to 3 rounds / 20 trials of an LLM repair
  loop that edits the RTL to pass the run's SHIPPED CHECKS (assertion-like checks over simulation traces,
  generated by the pipeline; golden was never visible to it).

Quantitatively the arms are indistinguishable on behaviour (agreement with the golden design, `docs/evidence/e7/BEHAVIOUR.md`):
repair raises the shipped-check pass rate (+8.5 pts) but not agreement with golden; spreads are the same.
**Your job: find what the errors actually ARE, and whether the two arms differ QUALITATIVELY** -- in kind,
location, cause or style -- even if not in amount. Test the hypotheses below with evidence; add your own.

## Hypotheses to test (report each: SUPPORTED / REFUTED / INCONCLUSIVE, with numbers and examples)
- **H1 visibility.** Repaired designs' residual errors sit where the shipped checks are silent (no failing/
  deciding check for that port and scenario), while generated designs' errors include check-visible ones.
- **H2 reference-model pull.** Repair moves a design toward the RUN'S REFERENCE MODEL's reading (including its
  misreadings), not toward golden. Seed data: ref-model agreement rises r0 -> final in 9 of 15 jobs while golden
  agreement falls in 6 of them. Test: at edges where r0 and final differ, does final agree with golden, the ref
  model (the `ref` rows of the stim file for that run's own stimulus), both or neither? What behaviour changed?
- **H3 common mode.** The main errors are shared across designs and arms (one misreading of the spec, or one
  generator habit). Find the top error modes and their prevalence per arm; tie each to the spec text (ambiguous
  sentence vs plain mistake vs golden doing something the spec does not say).
- **H4 regressions vs fixes.** What does repair fix, and what does it break that r0 had right?
- **H5 patching style.** Diff `rtl_generated.sv` vs `rtl.sv`: local special cases, timing/latency tweaks, or
  structural rewrites? Is the repaired code qualitatively different from the ablation samples?
- **H6 timing vs function.** In closed loop, are repaired designs' residual errors more timing-like (dedup-equal)
  or more functional than generated ones?
