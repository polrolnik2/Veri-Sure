# or1200_sb: qualitative error analysis (luna12/13/14)

The golden design is a pure pass-through: `OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED` is undefined, and golden traces equal the wired-through inputs on all 18,849 edges. Eight of the 15 designs are equivalent to it. Across all 15 designs, 80,961 edges disagree with golden. 99.35% of them come from one cause: the design builds the enabled store-buffer configuration. The remaining 0.65% come from luna14.gen1. Scripts, directed testbenches and logs are in `qual/or1200_sb/`. That folder includes a closed-loop directed bench (DC master and a Wishbone-like slave, 8 scenarios) run on every design, on the three Python ref models, and on golden compiled with `+define+OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED`. I wrote the `or1200_sb_fifo` for that last run from memory of OpenCores, because it is not in the repo, so its results are only indicative.

Behaviour shared by every buffering design and every ref, so not a divergence between them:
- cyc/stb are forced to 1 on the SB path. The spec says nothing on this; the source's enabled branch uses `outstanding_store`.
- A BIU error on a buffered store is retried forever and never reported. This follows the spec literally: only `sbbiu_ack_i` clears outstanding_store, and dcsb_err_o is forced to 0.
- FIFO depth: 4 in all designs, but 16 in ref12. The spec is silent. In S8, ref12 accepts 10 writes before stalling, where the designs accept 4 (5 with the bypass).
- Reads wait until the buffer drains.

## 2. Hypotheses

- **H1 visibility: SUPPORTED, in an inverted form.**
  - The repaired finals have 0 failing checks (luna12: 64 pass, 4 abstain; luna13: 65 pass, 1 abstain), yet 74.8% of their edges disagree with golden. Every residual error is invisible to the checks, because the checks encode mode A.
  - The only error the checks can see belongs to the generated arm (luna14.gen1, mode B).
  - The 7 golden-equivalent generated designs fail 6–7 checks each. The visible failures are failures of correct behaviour.
- **H2 reference-model pull: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
  - 70–80% of testpoints do not start with reset. In the open-loop simulation they inherit the previous testpoint's state, while the `ref` rows assume a fresh model. This drags BEHAVIOUR's `ref` down (0.62 for luna12.final, 0.50 for luna13.final). It is a methodological caveat for stateful modules.
  - Counting only edges after the first reset, ref agreement goes from 0.805 to 0.988 in luna12 and from 0.500 to 0.999 in luna13 (r0 to final).
  - Post-reset edges where r0 and final differ: luna12 has 332, where final matches the ref on 328 and golden on 2, and r0 had matched golden on 290. luna13 has 504, where final matches the ref on 503 and r0 had matched golden on 503.
  - Directed closed loop: luna13.final is identical to ref13 in 7/7 scenarios. luna12.final is identical to ref14 in 7/7 and to ref12 in 6/7; the one difference is depth. luna12.final is 99.4% trace-identical to luna12.gen1, the spec-literal sample.
  - Before repair, luna12.r0's write sequence matched the reconstructed source SB in 5/7 scenarios; after repair, only 1/7.
- **H3 common mode: SUPPORTED.** Mode A alone accounts for 99.35% of the gap. It is grouped by run, not by arm: luna12's generator never emitted the `ifdef` (0/4), while luna13/14 kept it (8/8). I could not find out why. The contract shapes look the same in all three runs.
- **H4 regressions vs fixes: SUPPORTED as pure regression.** Repair fixed nothing relative to golden.
  - luna13: golden edge agreement fell from 1.000 to 0.253.
  - luna12: it fell from 0.354 to 0.252; the exactly-once bypass was replaced by mode A1.
  - luna14: the outputs did not change.
- **H5 patching style: different in kind.**
  - luna12: a local reordering of the FIFO update (the bypass was demoted).
  - luna13: a one-line `` `define OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED`` inside the module, plus a `fifo_last` register copied from the ref.
  - luna14: a 50-line shadow FIFO in the `else` branch that drives only the probes (fifo_wr, fifo_full, fifo_wr_ack). Its pass rate rose from 0.424 to 0.470 with outputs untouched. Its log reasons: "I can't unconditionally define it, or I risk violating disabled behavior."
  - Generated samples never contain a macro override or probe-only logic.
- **H6 timing vs function: REFUTED.**
  - Closed loop, repaired finals: 91% of testpoints differ functionally and 0.1% differ only in timing (dedup-equal).
  - Generated buffering designs: 82–91% functional, about 0% timing-only.
  - The only timing-like errors are luna14.gen1's (7.7% of testpoints dedup-equal).

## 3. Qualitative difference between the arms

There is one. Errors in the generated arm come from the generator: one run's habit of hard-wiring the enabled branch, and ad-hoc FIFO bugs (A3/A4). Their buffering details vary: exactly-once, first-store duplicate, stale slot, lost store.

Repair removes that variety. It converges on the run's reference model, bugs included: A1 in luna12, A2 in luna13, at about 99% after reset. It acts on the **configuration and observability layer**: one repair defines the macro, another fakes probe activity. The ablation samples never do either. Repair moved behaviour toward the ref model's misreading and never toward golden.

## 4. Justifiable readings

1. **The configuration rule closes 99.35% of the gap** (80,434 of 80,961 edges; 6 of 7 non-equivalent designs).
   - The spec conditions behaviour on a named preprocessor macro and describes both branches: "When OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED is enabled…" and "When OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED is not defined, the module does not instantiate the Store Buffer FIFO…".
   - The reading this licenses: implement both branches under `` `ifdef``, never define the macro inside the module, and tie the probes to constants in the disabled branch.
   - Under Verilog semantics a macro nobody defines is undefined. So the as-delivered behaviour is pass-through, and this is the only reading that needs no invented fact.
   - The other cues ("is a Store Buffer module", the list of internal signals) favour *enabled*. But they describe what the module contains, not which build is active.
   - A supporting convention from outside the spec: the OR1200 distribution's `or1200_defines.v` ships `` //`define OR1200_SB_IMPLEMENTED`` commented out.
   - **Verdict:** whether the evaluated build defines the macro is not decidable from the text. "Keep the switch, define nothing" is decidable, and it yields golden. Nine designs followed it. Eight are golden-equivalent; the ninth, luna14.gen1, has a separate bug (reading 2).
   - The pipeline encoded the opposite: the ref default `True`, population voting, and probe obligations.
2. **"Directly" means combinational, which closes 0.65%** (527 edges). The quoted "directly forwards / directly returned" sentences leave no room for a register. Only fifo_wr_ack is "generated by a register".
3. **Exactly-once issue: 0% of the golden gap, so it matters only for enabled builds.** It rests on "sent one by one to the BIU" and "only one uncompleted Store Buffer write transaction". It would have rejected A1–A4, but the spec gives no FIFO empty-bypass mechanism.

**What I could not establish:**
- Why luna12's generator dropped the `ifdef`.
- The exact testpoints where the REQ-0034 check convicted r0. Those testpoints carry state over in the replay traces.
- That my reconstructed `or1200_sb_fifo` matches the real one.
