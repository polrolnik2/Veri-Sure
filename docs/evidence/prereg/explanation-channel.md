# PRE-REGISTERED: does the CHECK'S OWN THEORY hurt the editor?

Fixed before dispatch. **This exists to falsify a claim of mine**, not to confirm
one.

## The claim under test, and its current evidential status

§9af concluded: *"a locally-correct objection from a globally-wrong check carries
a globally-wrong EXPLANATION, and the editor acts on explanations."* That is an
**inference from one run plus the editor's self-report** — exactly the shape of
evidence this plan has retracted mechanism claims for before (the REQ-0069
regression-net reading, among others). It must be tested directly or withdrawn.

§9af named **two** candidate mechanisms and the run cannot separate them:

1. **the explanation channel** — the check's `detail` is its author's theory, and
   a check unsound suite-wide carries a wrong theory even where its verdict is
   right;
2. **volume** — 155 objections may simply exceed what a 21-trial budget can
   prioritise, whatever they say.

## The experiment, which isolates (1) by holding everything else fixed

The **same** ceiling set, the **same** mask, the **same** 155 starting
objections, the **same** start design, stimulus and budget. One thing differs:
`NODETAIL` replaces each objection's `said` field — the check's account of what
is wrong — with the **ports that check reads**, so the editor is told WHERE to
look and not WHAT to conclude.

**It remains a CEILING and is not a golden-free score.** But the PRESCRIPTION it
would license is golden-free: *strip check-authored theories from the editor's
feed* is a pipeline change requiring no reference, applicable to the golden-free
set directly.

## The bar

The comparison is against the ceiling arm's **261 of 348**, with the golden-free
DEDUP arm at 174 and the five control draws at 146–220.

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| **< 220** — better than every control | the explanation channel was a major contributor. §9af's mechanism stands, and the prescription is to suppress check-authored theory and keep localisation |
| **220–260** | directional only. Report the number beside 261 and build nothing on it |
| **>= 261** — no better than with explanations | **§9af's mechanism is REFUTED and I withdraw it.** Volume, or something neither run isolates, explains the ceiling's poor result, and the "editors consume explanations" sentence comes out of the scorecard |

**A fall in objections is again not a result** — only the miter and the testpoint
count are comparable, both computed last.
