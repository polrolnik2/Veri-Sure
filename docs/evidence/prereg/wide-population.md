# PRE-REGISTERED: the widened population

Fixed before any number is read. Written because every golden-free figure on
this plan -- set blindness, the minority rule, the corpus closure at the level
of catches (§9q) and at the level of opinion (§9aa) -- is measured against a
population of NINE designs, and twenty more designs produced by this pipeline
sit on disk with complete trace sets.

## What is being added, and what it is

29 designs have complete 348-testpoint trace sets:

* **7 written independently** from the specification, each by an agent forbidden
  to open any other design -- B, C, D, E, F, G, H. These are the population's
  original members.
* **22 LOOP-ACCEPTED** -- each one an editor run driven to its own stopping
  point against this criterion or a variant of it. Two (FULL, SD) are already in
  the population; the other twenty are not.

**MEASURED FIRST, BEFORE THE BARS BELOW WERE WRITTEN: all 29 are behaviourally
distinct**, on declared outputs and on outputs-plus-probes alike. No two collapse.
That does NOT contradict §9k's "five opinions, not nine" -- §9k is measured **at
the 812 blind split cells**, where B, D, E, F and H agree. Distinct over a whole
trace and identical where the checks are blind are different facts, and the
second is the one §9k is about. Nothing here revises it.

## Why loop-accepted designs belong in the population

§9b: *"The designs your own checks accepted belong in the population, and they
are the valuable ones... adding them moves the estimate 42.9% -> 55.1%, twelve
points. Whatever a set could see was edited out of them, so what remains is
exactly what it cannot -- adversarial selection for free."*

**The circularity is the point and is stated rather than hidden.** These designs
were accepted BY this criterion, so the set is guaranteed to be blind on them.
That is what makes them the right instrument for measuring blindness, which is
the definition of what a set cannot see. It is also why they cannot be used to
argue the set is GOOD -- only to bound how bad it is.

## THE CORRELATION HAZARD, AND THE THRESHOLD RULE IT FIXES

**Twenty of the twenty-nine are edits of ONE start design** (`gen/L.v`). They are
not independent draws. A check that catches a defect L has would convict every
one of its descendants and be REJECTED as over-strict -- the minority rule's
premise inverted by correlation, and it would silently discard exactly the most
valuable checks.

**So the minority threshold is NOT scaled to the widened population, and is not
applied over it.** It stays at `convicts <= 2` over the NINE it was calibrated on
(95 of 95 on its reject side). The widened population is used for the NEW-VECTOR
and EVER-OBJECTS legs only -- the legs that detect a new OPINION, which is what
widening is for. Any figure that applies a minority rule across all 29 is a
different measurement and is not quotable as this one.

## Q1 -- DOES THE CORPUS HOLD A SOUND OPINION ONLY A WIDER POPULATION REVEALS?

§9aa closed the corpus at the level of opinion **on nine designs**, and named its
own limit: *"A sixteenth design could make a currently-mute vector object."*
There are twenty. This tests that caveat directly.

Three reference-free filters, in order, over every corpus body outside the set:

    NEW           its verdict vector over the 29 is not one the set already holds
    EVER-OBJECTS  it objects somewhere on the 29 -- a mute vector closes no cell
    MINORITY      it convicts at most 2 of the NINE (see the threshold rule above)

The audit -- how many survivors convict the reference -- is computed LAST and
selects nothing.

**Q1 is not circular.** The corpus bodies played no part in accepting these
designs; the question is whether a body the SET does not contain says something
new about them.

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| **>= 1 survivor SPARES the reference** | **the nine-design closure was POPULATION-LIMITED.** Widening the population is a real and free lever on blindness -- the pipeline produces these designs anyway -- and the route is to add loop-accepted designs and re-select. Report which requirements the survivors span and how many are new to the set |
| **0 survivors spare the reference** | **the closure is robust to a 3.2x wider, adversarially-selected population.** Strictly stronger than the nine-design form: there is nothing sound left in this corpus to select, at the level of catches or of opinions, against any population this pipeline produces. That is the strongest golden-free assurance available here, and it closes selection for good |

## Q2 -- WHAT IS SET BLINDNESS ON THE POPULATION THE PIPELINE ACTUALLY PRODUCES?

Report the 29-design figure beside the nine-design 55.1%, **stratified by port
width** (§9v found the instrument inverts on wide ports; unstratified it is not
quotable) and **with the per-pair spread beside the aggregate** (§9b: blindness
zero on a small population is the same shape of evidence as a check that passes
because it never fired).

**PRE-REGISTERED DIRECTION: §9b's mechanism predicts blindness goes UP**, because
loop-accepted designs have had whatever the set could see edited out of them. A
rise confirms the mechanism and means 55.1% was optimistic. **A fall would refute
§9b's mechanism and must be explained before anything else here is read.**

**Q2 IS A REPORTING CHANGE, NOT A SELECTION CHANGE**, exactly as §9v's width
correction was. It must not be quoted as progress.

## WHAT NEITHER QUESTION CAN DO

Neither raises the graded score. Equivalence is unmet at **146 of 348 testpoints
on the best of five graded draws**, and nothing measured here changes that. Q1's
positive outcome would NAME a lever; it would not have pulled it. Q2 is
calibration. Reporting either as progress toward equivalence is the defect this
plan has retracted ten headlines for.

## INTEGRITY PINS, each a defect already made on this plan

* **The reference is not in the enumeration** and the script refuses if it is named.
* **The start design L is excluded** -- every loop-accepted design is an edit of
  it, so counting it too double-counts the lineage's shared reading.
* **The audit runs LAST** and selects nothing.
* **The minority threshold is not scaled** -- see the threshold rule above.
* **Blindness is stratified by port width or not quoted.**
* **`Rows(..., mode=)` is `direct` for every generated design**; `decode` is for
  the reference only. Decoding a generated design's states with the reference's
  numbering is the leak the probe architecture exists to avoid.
