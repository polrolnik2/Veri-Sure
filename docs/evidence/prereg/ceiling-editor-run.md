Labelled a ceiling exactly as the MAXSOUND run and E0's reference-derived probes

## The question nothing has answered

Everything measured so far shows that **no golden-free RULE** separates the good
objections from the bad — thirteen instruments, the last (§9ae) plateauing at 52%
precision across its whole range. **Nothing has ever tested whether the CHECKS
THEMSELVES suffice.** That is a different question and it has a decisive answer
either way:

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| **`NO-DIFF-40`** | **the checks ARE sufficient.** The entire gap is the selection rule, and what a production pipeline needs is a better separator — a concrete, bounded target, not an open research problem. The finish condition is reachable in principle and the golden-free question is the only thing between here and it |
| **`DIFFERS`** | **the checks are INSUFFICIENT.** Even with per-testpoint knowledge of exactly where each objection is correct — knowledge no pipeline can have — the corpus cannot drive an editor to equivalence. No rule could ever have helped, and the approach is closed at its root rather than at its instrument |

## Integrity pins

* **The audit was VERIFIED by re-scoring the reference through the mask**, not
  inferred from the construction. "True by construction" has been wrong on this
  plan before.
* **The mask is applied at the single decision site in the driver**, and with it
  unset the driver is byte-identical — confirmed by re-running the previous arm
  and reading the same `0 of 87`.
* Same start design, same stimulus, same 21-trial budget as every graded draw.
* Graded in its **own clean directory** from the accepted `dut.v`, three pins in
  one process, audit last.
* The editor is told nothing about the mask and is under the same no-reference
  restrictions as every other arm.

## What a DIFFERS outcome would NOT license

It would not say the specification is unimplementable, nor that the editor is
weak. It says **this corpus of checks, given perfect per-testpoint scoping, does
not contain enough constraint to force equivalence** — which is a statement about
the checks and the stimulus, and it is the one that decides whether any further
work on selection is worth doing.
